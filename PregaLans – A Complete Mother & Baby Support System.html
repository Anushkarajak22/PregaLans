Introduction & Problem Statement

🚑

Medical emergencies

Pregnancy can involve unexpected medical emergencies.

⏱️

Slow ambulance access

Families may struggle to arrange an ambulance quickly.

💊

Medicines & checkups

Finding prescribed medicines and home checkups can be difficult.

📇

Missing contact details

Important pregnancy and family contact details may not be available during an emergency.

There is a need for one simple platform for pregnancy support.

“Every minute matters when a mother needs medical care.”

Proposed Solution

A single website for pregnancy-related support services.

  • Emergency ambulance request through a simple form.
  • Online requests for prescribed medicines.
  • Home health checkup booking starting at a proposed ₹500.
  • Secure storage of pregnancy and family contact details.
  • A simple dashboard to access important services.
“One website. Multiple services. Better-connected care.”

Key Features

🚑

Emergency Ambulance

Request urgent transport and share the patient’s location and contact details.

📋

Pregnancy Profile

Store the expected delivery date, pregnancy stage, allergies and other relevant details.

💊

Medicine Delivery

Request doctor-prescribed medicines for delivery.

🩺

Home Checkups

Book a proposed ₹500 basic visit for BP and other available checks.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Connect

Save the husband’s and trusted family members’ contact details.

📅

Appointment Booking

Request a visit from a qualified healthcare professional.

“From emergency support to everyday care, everything starts in one place.”

How the Website Works

Register using basic contact details.
Add pregnancy information and expected delivery date.
Add a husband or trusted family contact, with their consent.
Choose ambulance, medicines or a home checkup.
Submit the request and receive a demo confirmation.
In a real service, the verified provider confirms the booking and communicates the next steps.
“Simple steps reduce confusion when families need support.”

“Simple web technologies can create a foundation for connected maternal care.”

Benefits & Social Impact

  • Saves time by bringing multiple service requests together.
  • Helps families access important pregnancy information.
  • Offers a proposed affordable home-checkup option.
  • Makes it easier to coordinate with trusted family members.
  • Helps healthcare providers receive organized booking information.
  • Could support families in areas with limited access to nearby services, if local partners are available.
“We are not just building a website; we are building a support system for mothers.”

Future Scope & Conclusion

📍 Live ambulance tracking and estimated arrival time
🏥 Verified hospital and ambulance partner network
🔐 Secure login for patients and authorized family members
💳 Online payments and medicine-order tracking
⏰ Appointment reminders and pregnancy checkup schedules
🗣️ Regional-language support for easier access
🌾 Expansion to more cities and rural areas
“PregnaCare aims to make pregnancy support more accessible, organized and family-connected.”
